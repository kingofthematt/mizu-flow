Three truths about hydration

01

Your brain runs on water

Even mild dehydration — as little as 1–2% of body weight — can impair focus, memory, and mood. A well-hydrated mind stays sharp when it matters most.

02

Energy lives in every cell

Water transports nutrients, regulates temperature, and keeps joints lubricated. Without enough of it, fatigue sets in long before thirst does.

03

Consistency beats volume

Sipping steadily throughout the day outperforms chugging a bottle once. Small, regular habits build lasting hydration — exactly what Mizu Flow is built for.
